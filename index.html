DEPOT DAYS '27

​Depot Days is an annual, weeklong artist retreat affiliated with the glitch.cool collective in upstate NY, hosted by Alex Gohorel (aka oddlogic) and his wife Jessie Green at their home. Activities consist of largely self-directed creative work (studio/listening sessions, open decks, workshops, etc.), off-site visits (other creative spaces in the area, hiking/swimming, farmers markets, etc.), and lots of time spent cooking, talking shop, and eating together. We aim for the experience to be casual and relaxing, while still being creatively engaging and inspiring.
